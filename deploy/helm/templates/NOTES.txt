{{ if not .Values.networkPolicy.enabled -}}

   and reachable by any pod in the cluster. Enable in production:
     networkPolicy.enabled: true
   Ensure the ingress controller namespace label matches:
     ingressControllerNamespaceSelector: {{ .Values.networkPolicy.ingressControllerNamespaceSelector | toJson }}

{{ end -}}
{{ if .Values.ingress.enabled -}}
⚠  Ingress is enabled. The /metrics endpoint will be exposed externally
   unless you restrict it via a NetworkPolicy or an ingress annotation.
   Recommended: networkPolicy.enabled=true restricts /metrics to the
   Prometheus namespace only.

{{ end -}}
{{ if .Values.authorizationPolicy.enabled -}}
ℹ  The Istio AuthorizationPolicy needs a sidecar on the API pods and on every caller, with mutual TLS
   between them: the mesh names each caller by its service account, so a caller without a mesh identity
   is refused on the internal paths (/api/v1/internal/*). Enforcing mutual TLS mesh-wide
   (PeerAuthentication) is Platform's, not this chart's. The caller service accounts and namespaces in
   authorizationPolicy.internalRoutes are placeholders to confirm with Platform.

{{ else -}}
⚠  authorizationPolicy.enabled is false: only the NetworkPolicy stands in front of the internal routes,
   and any pod it admits can send the x-user-id and x-tenant-roles headers the service checks (they are
   not a credential). The Istio policy needs a sidecar and mutual TLS; PeerAuthentication is Platform's.

{{ end -}}
